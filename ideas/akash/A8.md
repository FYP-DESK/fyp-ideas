## F-1: Zaak KYC — One-verify, share-everywhere KYC hub

**Problem:** Every Pakistani bank, exchange, telecom, and fintech re-asks for the same CNIC, biometric, address proof. A user doing 3 KYC verifications in a month.repeat the same paper work 3 times. Institutions each pay a verification fee (NADRA e-KYC ~PKR 500–1,000 per check as a certificate fee, not a pure API call; real per-verification cost is opaque). For a gig worker or saver opening accounts across 4 platforms, this is fatigue + time + friction.

**Solution:** A user-verified identity wallet. User does one thorough KYC with a partner institution (or a dummy NADRA simulator for the FYP). The result is stored as a cryptographically signed "KYC attestation" (JSON Web Token signed by the verifying institution + a hash anchored to a private Ethereum-compatible chain or just a tamper-evident audit log for the FYP). When another institution needs KYC, they request a ZK-proof-style claim: "Is this person a verified adult Pakistani resident?" without seeing the raw data. For the FYP MVP: 3 simulated institutions (dummy Bank A, dummy Exchange B, dummy Telecom C), a user wallet app, and a verifier portal.

**Story:** Bilal, a rider for a food app, earns enough to open a bank account, join a freelance payment platform, and verify on a crypto P2P app. Today he walks into a bank branch, gets his CNIC photocopied, then downloads 3 different apps each asking for the same selfie+live photo. With Zaak, he does KYC once at Bank A. Bank A issues a signed attestation. He opens the wallet app, shows a QR to Exchange B — B verifies in 30 seconds without seeing his CNIC image. He opens Telecom C — same. The 30-minute ordeal becomes 5 minutes.

**Scope:** Mobile app (user wallet) + web portal (institution verifier) + simple on-chain/private-ledger attestation layer. 3 dummy institutions for FYP.

**Feasibility:** Medium. The crypto/ZK part can be replaced with signed JWT + audit log for the FYP. The hard part is the legal/signature model — solved for FYP by using a simulator. Real deployment needs SBP/further licensing.

**Uniqueness:** Pakistan has no consumer-facing "self-sovereign KYC wallet." Most KYC is institution-internal. This flips it.

**Value:** Reduces verification time from days to seconds; reduces per-institution re-verification cost; user owns their data.

**MVP:** User registers → does dummy KYC with Institution A → gets attestation QR → shows it to Institution B and C → B and C approve. Demo in 2-week sprint.

## F-2: MicroBun — micro-investment pool into Pakistan Investment Bonds

**Problem:** Pakistan Investment Bonds (PIBs) and T-Bills have high minimums (often PKR 100,000+). Regular salaried people — the same people using Easypaisa and JazzCash — cannot access risk-free government securities. Banks pay 1–2% on savings accounts while inflation has frequently been higher. The gap between "retail savers" and "government bond market" is large.

**Solution:** A mobile app that pools small investments from users into a single T-Bill/PIB purchase via a partner bank or distributor. Users get fractional "certificates" showing their share of the bond, current value, and maturity payout. The app shows projected returns vs. a regular savings account. For the FYP: simulate the bond purchase via a dummy bank API; the core is the pooling + allocation + reporting logic.

**Story:** Sana, a school teacher in Faisalabad, saves PKR 2,000/month. She knows about government bonds from the news but has never bought one — the minimum is too high. MicroBun lets her and 499 others each put in PKR 2,000; the app pools PKR 1,000,000 and buys a T-Bill. Sana's app shows "Your share: PKR 2,000 / total pool: PKR 1,000,000 / maturity: PKR 2,070 in 3 months." She gets bond returns for the first time.

**Scope:** Mobile app + web dashboard + dummy bank integration for FYP. Real version needs a licensed distributor partner.

**Feasibility:** Easy–Mmedium. No AI. The hard part is regulatory (needs a distributor license); the FYP uses a simulator. The pooling math and reporting are straightforward.

**Uniqueness:** Fractional government bond access for Pakistani retail — not currently available as a consumer app.

**Value:** Opens a risk-free investment class to millions of low-income savers; drives financial inclusion.

**MVP:** 5 simulated users each deposit PKR 1,000 → pool = PKR 5,000 → dummy T-Bill purchased → maturity distribution shown. 1-week sprint.
